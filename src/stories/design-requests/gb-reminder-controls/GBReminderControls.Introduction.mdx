# Group Block Reminder Controls

Design for [PP-42, PP-43 and PP-44](https://linear.app/eventpipe/project/group-block-reminder-controls-906d7f0e4189),
from Scott Villemain's 09/04 Loom, *Group Block Reminders UI and Modal*.

> "It consists of two pretty simple UI changes." — Scott, 0:07

---

## The problem

Group block reminders are **hard-coded**: a confirmation when the block is
created, a reminder 2 days later, then **every 3 days until the release date**.
There is no way to change any of it, and no way to turn it off.

A housing company that lets blocks stay open longer therefore gets
proportionally more reminder email, aimed at the people who created those
blocks. At least one customer has resorted to **swapping the block contact email
to their own address and back** to suppress them — a workaround that quietly
breaks every other email that contact should receive.

Teams Management Communications Phase 2 makes this sharper: compliance reminders
can now be configured to send to the same group block contacts, so the same
people can end up on two independent reminder schedules that know nothing about
each other.

## The two changes

**1 · A setting on the event** — Edit Event › Email Settings gains an on/off
toggle and an interval, so a Hoco can stop the reminders or slow them down for
one event. Defaults preserve today's behavior exactly: on, every 3 days.

**2 · A warning when the two overlap** — saving Registration Settings with both
compliance reminders and group block reminders on raises an advisory modal, with
a way to turn group block reminders off as part of that save.

## What was deliberately not built

**Cross-screen state.** The modal's primary action turns group block reminders
off, which in the product also flips the toggle on Email Settings. Scott, at
3:00: *"You don't have to simulate that. I just need a mock of this page."* So
the two screens hold their own state and the modal reports what it did. PP-44's
acceptance criteria do require the two to stay in sync in the build.

**PP-45.** Reporting work, owned outside the application design.
